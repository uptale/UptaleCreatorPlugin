# Workflow

## 1. Start Choice

Ask whether the user wants to start from:

- An existing document.
- Zero.

For an existing document, wait for upload and confirmation before extracting anything.

## 2. Framing

Collect and validate:

- Experience type: training, assessment, or roleplay.
- Training need: business, strategic, or operational reason.
- Learners: role, level, prerequisites.
- Topic context: sector, real location, constraints.
- Pedagogical objectives, ideally no more than three per module.
- Key messages.
- Tone and desired emotion.
- Delivery context: classroom, remote, web, headset, mobile, tablet, or PC.
- Expected gains and KPIs.
- Evaluation and success criteria.
- Production and delivery constraints: target duration, scene count, 360 media preference, network, device limits.

For a document flow, propose framing answers from the confirmed document, then ask the user to validate, correct, or complete each point.

For a zero flow, ask questions directly without pretending to infer.

## 3. Pre-Storyboard

Use only when starting from zero. Collect one item at a time:

- Pedagogical content.
- Associated real situation.
- Corresponding real location.

After each item, reformulate in a mini-table and ask whether to add another item.

## 4. Storytelling

Produce a continuous immersive narrative with:

- Learner role.
- Secondary roles.
- Hook.
- Detailed story with transitions.
- Ending or endings.

For roleplay, clarify whether it is training or assessment before branching deeply.

## 5. Experience Map

Generate a visual flowchart only. Represent:

- Introduction and conclusion.
- Linear scenes.
- Decisions, quiz, microphone, or choice moments.
- Good and bad endings when relevant.
- Door or condition labels on transitions where useful.

## 6. Scene-By-Scene Detail

For each scene:

- Show one scene at a time.
- Include environment, activity, narrative summary, success criterion.
- Ask for validation.
- Offer tag table and 360 mockup.
- Apply corrections locally and re-display the scene before advancing.

## 7. Final Validation

After all scenes are validated, ask the user to validate the complete storyboard or identify scenes to correct.

Check:

- Coverage of all validated knowledge, situations, and locations.
- Coherence with the experience type.
- Coherence of feedback and alternative endings.
- Target duration and scene count.
- Criteria by scene and global criteria if defined.

## 8. Deliverables

After final validation, hand off to `uptale-deliverables` and `uptale-media-production-plan`.
